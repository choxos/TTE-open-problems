An unobservable protocol component produces a different, data-defined estimand rather than a noisy version of the intended one

PRO-04 Partially addressed Very high priority Promising Tractability 4/5

Bites on: Cross-cutting
Seen in: Any setting

Statement

The canonical protocol has seven linked components: eligibility, treatment strategies, assignment, follow-up, outcomes, causal contrast and analysis plan. Conceptual specification and operational realization are distinct, and when a required component cannot be observed the analyst does not obtain a mismeasured version of the intended effect. They obtain a different effect, defined by what the data can express. A criterion that cannot be verified is dropped and the population changes; a strategy that cannot be distinguished from its neighbor is merged and the contrast changes; an outcome that is only partly captured redefines the endpoint. Nothing in current reporting requires that substitution to be named, so the paper states the intended estimand and reports the substituted one.

Why it is open

The substitution happens component by component during implementation, often in code rather than in prose, and by the time results exist there is no artifact recording which component was compromised. Naming the substituted estimand requires a language for partially specified interventions that does not yet exist in a usable form. There is also a reasonable disagreement underneath: some substitutions are innocuous refinements and others change the question, and no criterion separates them.

What has been tried

Work What it contributes
Hernán MA, Robins JM. Using big data to emulate a target trial when a randomized trial is not available. Am J Epidemiol 2016;183(8):758-764 Establishes the seven components and the requirement to map each to the data, which is the framework within which the substitution becomes visible.
Moreno-Betancur M, Wijesuriya R, Carlin JB. The ideal trial: defining causal estimands that balance relevance and feasibility in target trial emulations and actual randomized trials. Epidemiology 2026;37(2):153-162 Argues that discrepancies between the ideal, target and actual trials should be made explicit, which is the general case of this specific substitution.
Wang, et al. An operational target trial emulation framework for causal inference using electronic health records. npj Digit Med 2026 Works through the operational realization of each component against record data, which is where the substitutions occur in practice.

Probable solution or research direction

Require a component-by-component mapping table in which each of the seven components carries its conceptual specification, its operational realization, and a statement of what changed between them. Where a component cannot be realized, require the substituted estimand to be written out rather than the intended one, so that the paper’s headline quantity is the one estimated. Develop criteria, tested against benchmarking data, for which substitutions historically preserved agreement with the corresponding trial and which did not.

Question. Literature and prior-art check
open / supported. Operational frameworks for mapping each protocol component onto record data exist, and a 2024 Frontiers in Pharmacology case study applies the estimand and target trial frameworks together to an external control analysis, which is the closest the corpus comes to naming the substituted estimand explicitly. None requires that the substituted estimand replace the intended one in the paper’s headline, and none supplies criteria for which substitutions preserve the question. The entry’s claim is about a reporting requirement rather than a method, and no such requirement appears in the reporting literature. Cites 10.3389/fphar.2024.1223858: Combines the estimand framework with the target trial framework so the realized estimand is stated, which is the practice the entry asks to be made general.. Cites 10.1038/s41746-026-02563-z: Works through the operational realization of each component, which is where substitutions occur..
GPT-5.6 Sol, technical and source-code lens
open / overstated. The statement treats all failures of observation as estimand substitution, which is incorrect. Depending on the measurement and identification structure, an operational proxy may still identify the intended effect through a valid measurement-error, missing-data, or validation model; it may define a different, well-defined causal effect; or it may identify only a statistical functional with no coherent intervention-based interpretation. Dropping an eligibility criterion, merging strategies, and redefining an endpoint change the target only when the analyst actually makes those substitutions. They are not logically forced by partial observation. Conversely, simply naming a substituted estimand does not make it causal or identified. Agreement with a benchmark trial cannot determine whether an estimand was preserved because different targets and compensating biases can yield similar estimates. Component-level conceptual-to-operational mapping is important and can be decision-critical, but the universal claim and very-high-severity framing are excessive.
Grok 4.5, recency lens
partially-solved / supported-with-caveat. Conceptual naming of substituted estimands is advanced by Moreno-Betancur ideal/target/actual trials and by operational TTE frameworks (Hernán–Robins components; Wang npj Digit Med 2026 operational EHR mapping). That is partial progress on making substitution visible. Missing pieces remain: a usable language for partially specified interventions, mandatory component-by-component conceptual vs operational tables with substituted estimand as headline, and criteria validated on benchmarking data for which substitutions preserve trial agreement. Not fully solved. Cites arXiv:2405.10026: Partial: explicit ideal vs target vs actual trial discrepancies so unobservable components can be named as changed estimands rather than measurement error; does not deliver component mapping tables, partial-intervention language, or benchmarking criteria for innocuous vs question-changing substitutions.. Cites 10.1038/s41746-026-02563-z: Partial: works through operational realization of protocol components against EHR data, the setting where component substitution occurs; does not require substituted estimands as headline quantities or validation criteria for which substitutions preserve trial agreement..
GLM-5.2, third independent lens
open / supported. The claim is internally consistent and pitched at the right level of generality. It argues that an unobservable protocol component produces a different estimand rather than a noisy version, and that current reporting does not require naming this substitution. The reason given—that the substitution happens in code, no artifact records it, and no usable language or criterion currently exists to separate innocuous from question-changing substitutions—supports the claim that this remains an open gap in practice.

How the evidence falls in time

68 papers in the reviewed corpus bear on this problem, published 2007 to 2026. Progress and a fresh assertion of openness land in the same year.

This problem is recurrent: three or more papers spanning at least eight years assert it independently. Sustained restatement by authors who mostly do not cite each other is the strongest evidence this reading can offer that a gap is real rather than one group’s framing.

It is also being closed in pieces: partial results come from more than one paper in more than one year, each covering a different part.
