PRO-01 Overstated High priority Emerging Tractability 3/5

Statement

Three trials are in play in any emulation and only one is usually named: the ideal trial that would answer the decision-maker’s question, the target trial the investigator specifies, and the emulation the data support. Specifying the target trial closely around the available data conceals the distance between the first two, and the paper reports one estimate as though the chain were tight. What is missing is not an additive decomposition; that generally does not exist, because the transitions change populations, interventions, outcomes and scales, so the discrepancies do not add. What is missing is a documented lineage: the change from the decision question to the operational causal estimand, the assumptions mapping that estimand to an observed-data functional, and the estimation uncertainty, each recorded separately so a reader can see which step carries the load. No convention requires it, and no computable measure of the distance between two estimands is in use.

Why it is open

The first transition is a change of scientific question rather than a statistical error, so it has no sampling distribution and no natural metric. Measuring the distance between estimands requires a common space in which to compare an intervention that was never implementable with one that was, and that space has to be constructed rather than estimated. The incentives also run the wrong way: making the drift explicit weakens the paper’s claim without changing its number, so nothing but a reporting requirement would produce it.

What has been tried

Work What it contributes
Moreno-Betancur M, et al. The ideal trial: defining causal estimands that balance relevance and feasibility in target trial emulations and actual randomized trials. arXiv 2024 Distinguishes ideal, target and actual trials and argues that every discrepancy between them should be made explicit. It frames the problem and does not supply the decomposition.
Hernán MA, Robins JM. Using big data to emulate a target trial when a randomized trial is not available. Am J Epidemiol 2016;183(8):758-764 Establishes the target trial as the object to be specified, which is the middle term of the three and the only one the framework requires be written down.
Hansford HJ, et al. Design and implementation of observational studies emulating a target trial. JAMA Netw Open 2026 Design guidance for the operational steps, acknowledging that the operational methods remain scenario dependent.

Probable solution or research direction

Require an estimand lineage in the protocol, recording each transition as a change in population, treatment version, outcome definition or follow-up, written before the estimate exists. Report differences in intervention support and in target-population composition as concrete measures of drift, since both are computable from the data at hand and neither requires the ideal trial to be feasible. Publish the lineage next to the estimate, and state explicitly that it is a record of separate changes rather than an additive attribution of error.

Verification

Verdict. Overstated. 2 independent auditors judged the claim as stated too strong. Emulations should separately document changes from the decision question to the operational causal estimand, the assumptions mapping that estimand to an observed-data functional, and estimation uncertainty. This lineage need not form an additive numerical decomposition when populations, interventions, outcomes, or scales differ.

The source said. “Three trials are in play in any emulation and only one of them is usually named. There is the ideal trial that would answer the decision-maker’s question, the target trial that the investigator specifies, and the emulation the data actually support. Specifying the target trial closely around the available data conceals the distance between the first two, and the paper reports a single estimate as though the chain were tight. What does not exist is a decomposition of the total discrepancy into its four transitions: from the ideal estimand to the feasible target estimand, from that to the identified observed-data functional, and from that to the estimator’s output. Without one, a reader cannot tell whether a surprising result reflects estimation error or the fact that the question quietly changed at the first step.”

What was wrong with it. A decomposition in the arithmetic sense is not available and in general cannot be: when the populations, interventions, outcomes or scales differ across the transitions, the discrepancies do not add. What is achievable, and what the entry should have asked for, is a documented lineage recording each change separately rather than a single number attributed across four terms.

Dissent. literature dissented (supported-with-caveat): The framing is now published rather than a preprint: the ideal-trial paper the entry cites as arXiv 2405.10026 appeared in Epidemiology in 2026, and the entry should cite the journal version. A 2026 American Journal of Epidemiology paper sets out the anatomy of a causal estimand for population health research, which is the vocabulary a lineage would be written in. Neither supplies the decomposition the entry asks for: a formal accounting that attributes total discrepancy across the four transitions, with a computable measure of estimand distance. The claim survives with its citation corrected and its neighbors named.

GPT-5.6 Sol, technical and source-code lens
open / overstated. A structured estimand lineage is a defensible reporting objective, but the proposed decomposition is not mathematically defined. The statement names four objects but enumerates only three transitions. Under valid identification, the observed-data functional equals the target causal estimand and is not a separate loss term. If identification assumptions fail, the resulting causal bias is generally not identified from the same data. Effects defined using different populations, interventions, outcomes, or scales also have no canonical additive distance. Differences in intervention support or population composition neither decompose nor necessarily bound the difference between effects without bridge and effect-heterogeneity assumptions. This is therefore an open reporting and formalization item, not a universally available decomposition whose absence prevents interpretation, and its asserted high severity is inflated.

However, the reason given for it being open notes that the common space required for comparison ‘has to be constructed rather than estimated,’ implying that the decomposition is structurally possible but simply has not been developed or standardized in current practice.
